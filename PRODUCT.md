## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: owners of South African small businesses with 1 to 50 staff, choosing their first or next accounting, payroll or HR system, usually without an IT person or consultant. They arrive with a job to get done (VAT201 every two months, payroll by the 7th, leave out of WhatsApp) and limited time, and they want to land on the right product or a short list quickly.

Also served: bookkeepers and accountants advising clients, and finance and HR managers at mid sized companies weighing ERP and payroll systems sold on quotation.

## Product Purpose

Toolveld ("veld" is the South African word for open country) is an independent register of business software for South Africa. It profiles 37 products across accounting, payroll, HR, CRM, ERP and project management, prices each one in rand with its VAT basis, checks it against local rules (SARS eFiling, VAT201, EMP201, EMP501, IRP5, UIF, SDL, ETI, POPIA, BCEA), and publishes in depth editorial reviews, written head to head comparisons, a rand price index and a Sage hub. Success on the homepage: a first time owner finds the right product or category within seconds.

## Positioning

The only guide that prices every product in rand with the VAT basis stated, dates every price it checked against the vendor's own page or price guide, and marks each product's gaps against South African tax and labour rules instead of hiding them. Rankings come only from moderated reader reviews, never from payment.

## Operating Context

Owners compare on a laptop at the office or on a phone between jobs. Decisions turn on monthly rand cost for their headcount, whether the product handles SARS returns, which bank feeds it supports, and whether their accountant already uses it. Many products (ERP, enterprise payroll) are sold only on quotation through local partners.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js 16 App Router site; runs on a bundled dataset with optional Supabase.
- Directory with filters, product profiles with editorial review, pricing, cost analytics and FAQs, written comparisons, shortlist of up to four, a four question finder, guides, price index (/prices), Sage hub (/sage), newsletter, admin.
- Prices: 17 of 37 products checked on vendor sources (16 and 17 Nov 2026); the rest are labelled "Price not yet checked". VAT basis is shown or stated as not given.
- Never invent reviews, ratings, testimonials, customer logos or prices. Ratings appear only after 5 published reader reviews; there are none yet.
- Commercial links must carry an Ad label and disclosure; there are no affiliate programmes yet.
- Light only. Copy uses no dashes or hyphen punctuation and avoids stock marketing words.

## Product Principles

1. Numbers over adjectives: show the rand figure, the date it was checked and the VAT basis.
2. Gaps stay visible: a missing SARS or POPIA capability is shown, not buried.
3. Trust before revenue: rankings and verdicts are never for sale, and every commercial link says so.
4. Fast to the right product: an owner should reach a fitting product or category in seconds.
5. Local first: South African rules, banks, partners and prices lead every comparison.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast at minimum, full keyboard use with visible focus, reduced motion respected, and every page usable at 375 pixels wide without sideways scrolling. Charts carry their numbers in an accessible table.
